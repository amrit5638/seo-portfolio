SEO & Digital Marketing Skills

A selection of the services and practical skills I use across website optimisation, local search and paid advertising projects.

⌕

On-Page SEO

Improving website pages so their content, structure and relevance better match user search intent.

  • Keyword and search intent research
  • Title tags and meta descriptions
  • Headings and content optimisation
  • Internal linking and anchor text
  • Content structure and FAQs
⚙

Technical SEO

Reviewing technical factors that affect how search engines discover, crawl and understand a website.

  • Indexing and crawl checks
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt review
  • Canonical and redirect checks
  • Mobile usability and page performance
  • Structured data review
◎

Local SEO

Supporting local visibility for businesses targeting specific cities, suburbs, service areas and customer locations.

  • Google Business Profile optimisation
  • Local keyword research
  • Local landing pages
  • Business information consistency
  • Local ranking and visibility monitoring
✎

Content & Link Building

Planning relevant content and improving the connections between pages and useful external resources.

  • SEO blog and article planning
  • Content briefs and optimisation
  • Topic and competitor research
  • Internal linking strategy
  • Relevant outreach and link acquisition

How I work
